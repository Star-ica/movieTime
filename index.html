FAQ

Is the video itself stored on BOT Chain?

No. Blockchains are not suited to storing video. BOT Chain stores titles, metadata, access rules and payments; each title holds a configurable external video URL for content the publisher is licensed to distribute.

Will I be charged automatically?

Never. Every payment is a transaction you sign. Subscriptions simply expire at a blockchain timestamp.

Which currencies are accepted?

The BOT native token and USDT on BOT Chain.

What if my subscription expires?

Free titles stay available; premium titles lock until you renew. Renewing early extends from your current expiry.
